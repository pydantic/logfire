## HIPAA

![Pydantic Logfire HIPAA Badge](https://badges.oneleet.com/badge/0192577e-6462-4160-b19b-59e6e6af422e?dark=true#only-dark){ width="150" }

Logfire is <a href="https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/laws-regulations/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">HIPAA</a> compliant.

HIPAA requires a <a href="https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Business Associate Agreement (BAA)</a> between you and us if your use of Logfire involves Protected Health Information (PHI). We offer a boilerplate BAA on our Growth plan and a custom BAA on any Enterprise plan (see <a href="https://pydantic.dev/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener">pricing</a>). To put one in place, email [sales@pydantic.dev](mailto:sales@pydantic.dev).

## GDPR

![Pydantic Logfire GDPR Badge](https://badges.oneleet.com/badge/fd18577a-ed97-40a9-afb2-d32a8c755d2a?dark=true#only-dark){ width="150" }
![Pydantic Logfire GDPR Badge](https://badges.oneleet.com/badge/fd18577a-ed97-40a9-afb2-d32a8c755d2a?dark=false#only-light){ width="150" }

Pydantic is GDPR compliant.

The <a href="https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj" target="_blank" rel="noopener">General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)</a> requires a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) between you and us when Logfire processes personal data on your behalf. Our standard <a href="https://pydantic.dev/legal/data-processing-addendum" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DPA</a> applies on every plan. It is part of the <a href="https://pydantic.dev/legal/terms-of-service" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Terms of Service</a>, so it takes effect with the Terms and needs no separate signature.

The DPA covers the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection. For transfers of personal data out of the European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland, it applies the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). For transfers out of the UK, it applies the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Our <a href="https://pydantic.dev/legal/cloud-subprocessors" target="_blank" rel="noopener">subprocessors page</a> lists the third parties that process data on our behalf. We give 14 days' notice of changes to that list, and you can subscribe to those notices by email. To keep your data in the EU, use our [EU Data Region](reference/data-regions.md). Enterprise customers can negotiate custom DPA terms: email [sales@pydantic.dev](mailto:sales@pydantic.dev).
